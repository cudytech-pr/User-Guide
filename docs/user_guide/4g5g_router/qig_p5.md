# Quick Installation Guide

Follow the instructions below to get your router ready for use fast and easy.

1. Install the cellular antennas if there are detachable ones.
    <img src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/Cudytech-pr/User-Guide/docs/images/4g5g_router/qig-p51.webp" alt="" width="500px" style="display: block; margin: 0 auto;"/>

2. With the gold contacts facing down,insert your SlM card(s) into the SlM slot(s) until you hear a clicking sound.

    !!! Note
        No hot-swapping. Please insert or remove the SlM card while the system is powered off.

3. Plug the power adapter to the router and press ON/OFF button. Wait for the system LED to turn solid on. This may take 2-5 minutes.
    <img src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/Cudytech-pr/User-Guide/docs/images/4g5g_router/qig-p52.webp" alt="" width="500px" style="display: block; margin: 0 auto;"/>

4. Check the Internet LED.
    - If the Internet LED is in blue or purple, the router is already connected to the cellular network.
    - If the Internet LED is in red or off, please continue with the following steps to configure the router.

5. Connect to the router through Wi-Fi or with an Ethernet cable.
    - Through WiFi: Connect your device to the router's Wi-Fi using the SSlD (network name) and Password printed on the product label.
        <img src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/Cudytech-pr/User-Guide/docs/images/4g5g_router/qig-p53.webp" alt="" width="500px" style="display: block; margin: 0 auto;"/>
    - With an Ethernet cable: Turn off the Wi-Fi on your device and connect it to the router's LAN port with an Ethernet cable.
        <img src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/Cudytech-pr/User-Guide/docs/images/4g5g_router/qig-p54.webp" alt="" width="500px" style="display: block; margin: 0 auto;"/>

6. Launch a browser, enter [cudy.net](https://cudy.net) (or *192.168.10.1*) in the address bar, and create an administrator password to log in.

7. Follow the step-by-step instructions to finish the *Quick Setup*.
    - Select your *Time Zone*. (Optional)Enable *Auto Update*. Click *Next*.
        <img src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/Cudytech-pr/User-Guide/docs/images/4g5g_router/qig-qs1.webp" alt="" width="500px" style="border: 2px solid #eee; display: block; margin: 0 auto;" />
    - Select your *SIM Slot* and *APN Profile*, and *Auto* is recommended. For other options, check with your SIM card provider. Click *Next*.
        <img src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/Cudytech-pr/User-Guide/docs/images/4g5g_router/qig-qs2.webp" alt="" width="500px" style="border: 2px solid #eee; display: block; margin: 0 auto;" />
    - Customize your 2.4G/5G Wireless SSID and password or just keep it as default. Click *Next*.
        <img src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/Cudytech-pr/User-Guide/docs/images/4g5g_router/qig-qs3.webp" alt="" width="500px" style="border: 2px solid #eee; display: block; margin: 0 auto;" />
    - Confirm your settings or click *Back* to make adjustments. Click *Save & Apply*.
        <img src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/Cudytech-pr/User-Guide/docs/images/4g5g_router/qig-qs4.webp" alt="" width="500px" style="border: 2px solid #eee; display: block; margin: 0 auto;" />
!!! Note
    - If the signal is still too weak indoors, try installing your extension antennas (not included in the box) to the SMA connector and placing the antennas outdoors.
    - If a *SIM blocked* window pops up, please input your SIM PIN or contact your carrier for help.
