# Quick Installation Guide

!!! Note
    RE3600 is used as an example in this guide. Refer to your actual product for more details.

Follow the instructions below to get your range extender ready for use fast and easy.

1. Plug the Extender into a socket near the router, and wait for it to boot up.
    <img src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/Cudytech-pr/User-Guide/docs/images/range_extender/qig01.webp" alt="" width="500px" style="display: block; margin: 0 auto;"/>

2. Pair the extender with the router using Method A or B.
    - Method A: Press the WPS buttons on the router and the Extender, and wait for the LED to light up.
        <img src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/Cudytech-pr/User-Guide/docs/images/range_extender/qig02.webp" alt="" width="500px" style="display: block; margin: 0 auto;"/>

    - Method B: Connect your phone or computer to the Extender's Wi-Fi. The setup page will automatically open in your browser. Follow the on-screen instructions to complete the Quick Setup.

        - Select *Wireless Extender* as the operation mode. Click *Next*.
            <img src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/Cudytech-pr/User-Guide/docs/images/range_extender/re_qs1.webp" alt="" width="300px" style="border: 2px solid #eee; display: block; margin: 0 auto;" />
        - Select your time zone from the drop-down list. Click *Next*.
            <img src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/Cudytech-pr/User-Guide/docs/images/range_extender/re_qs2.webp" alt="" width="300px" style="border: 2px solid #eee; display: block; margin: 0 auto;" />
        - Select an existing network your want to extend. Click *Connect*. 
            <img src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/Cudytech-pr/User-Guide/docs/images/range_extender/re_qs3.webp" alt="" width="300px" style="border: 2px solid #eee; display: block; margin: 0 auto;" />
        - Enter its password if required, and click *Next*. 
            <img src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/Cudytech-pr/User-Guide/docs/images/range_extender/re_qs4.webp" alt="" width="300px" style="border: 2px solid #eee; display: block; margin: 0 auto;" />
        - Wait for a few moments until successful connection.
            <img src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/Cudytech-pr/User-Guide/docs/images/range_extender/re_qs5.webp" alt="" width="300px" style="border: 2px solid #eee; display: block; margin: 0 auto;" />
        - Successfully connected. Click *Next*.
            <img src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/Cudytech-pr/User-Guide/docs/images/range_extender/re_qs6.webp" alt="" width="300px" style="border: 2px solid #eee; display: block; margin: 0 auto;" />
        - Set the extended network name (SSID) or just keep it as default. Passwords will be the same as the host network. Click *Next*.
            <img src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/Cudytech-pr/User-Guide/docs/images/range_extender/re_qs7.webp" alt="" width="300px" style="border: 2px solid #eee; display: block; margin: 0 auto;" />
        - Confirm your settings and click *Save & Apply*, or click *Back* to make adjustments. 
            <img src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/Cudytech-pr/User-Guide/docs/images/range_extender/re_qs8.webp" alt="" width="300px" style="border: 2px solid #eee; display: block; margin: 0 auto;" />

3. Connect your device to the extended Wi-Fi and verify Internet access. By default, the extended Wi-Fi uses the same password as your router's Wi-Fi. When paired via WPS, it uses the same Wi-Fi name (SSID) as your router.
    <img src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/Cudytech-pr/User-Guide/docs/images/range_extender/qig03.webp" alt="" width="500px" style="display: block; margin: 0 auto;"/>

4. Relocate the extender between the Wi-Fi coverage area and the area where you want to expand the Wi-Fi signal.
    <img src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/Cudytech-pr/User-Guide/docs/images/range_extender/qig04.webp" alt="" width="500px" style="display: block; margin: 0 auto;"/>
