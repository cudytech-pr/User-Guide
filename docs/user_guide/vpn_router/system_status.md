## Devices 
shows the number of devices connected to this router and their status being online or blocked. Click *More Details* to know more information about these devices or configure the settings.

<img src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/Cudytech-pr/User-Guide/docs/images/vpn_router/system_status_device_status.webp" alt="" width="800px" style="border: 1px solid #eee;" />

<img src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/Cudytech-pr/User-Guide/docs/images/vpn_router/system_status_device_settings.webp" alt="" width="800px" style="border: 1px solid #eee;" />

- Internet: Click to enable/disable the Internet for the devices.
- VPN: Click to enable/disable VPN for the devices.
- Operation: Click to pop up the editing page as below, where you can customize the device name and limit the download/upload rate.

## System 
shows the System information including firmware version, local time and uptime. Click *More Details* to view more information about it or make changes on Settings page.

- *Status* shows the Router info about firmware version, model, hardware version, local time and uptime.
    
    <img src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/Cudytech-pr/User-Guide/docs/images/vpn_router/system_status_system_status.png" alt="" width="800px" style="border: 1px solid #eee;" />

- *ARP* lists the connected devices' IP address with its bound MAC address, Hostname and Interfaces.
    
    <img src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/Cudytech-pr/User-Guide/docs/images/vpn_router/system_status_system_arp.png" alt="" width="800px" style="border: 1px solid #eee;" />

- *Active IP Routes* table contains a list of routes to network destinations associated with and known by the Router.
    
    <img src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/Cudytech-pr/User-Guide/docs/images/vpn_router/system_status_system_active_ip_routes.png" alt="" width="800px" style="border: 1px solid #eee;" />

- *System Log* tracks all the Router behaviors. It is used for [*technical support*](mailto:support@cudy.com) to diagnose and troubleshoot for the Router.  It is also available on *Diagnostic Tools -> System Log* page.
    
    <img src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/Cudytech-pr/User-Guide/docs/images/vpn_router/system_status_system_logs.png" alt="" width="800px" style="border: 1px solid #eee;" />

    - Update: Click to refresh the system log.
    - Clear: Click to erase all the system log up till now.
    - Download: Click to download the system log for technical support.
