# Install & Power

!!!Note
    Power off the router before installations

## Install Nano SIM Cards

1. Press at the SIM1/2 slot to pop out the SIM tray(s).
    <img src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/Cudytech-pr/User-Guide/docs/images/industrial_router/install-sim-ir021.webp"  width="500" alt="">

2. Put the Nano SIM card(s) in the SIM tray(s) as diagram shows.
    <img src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/Cudytech-pr/User-Guide/docs/images/industrial_router/install-sim-ir022.webp"  width="500" alt="">

3. Push the SIM tray(s) back into the SIM1/2 slot. Store the pin back into the SlM Pin slot.
    <img src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/Cudytech-pr/User-Guide/docs/images/industrial_router/install-sim-ir023.webp"  width="500" alt="">

---

## Install 4G and Wi-Fi Antenna(s)

1.  Assign the 4G antennas to DIV and MAIN connectors, and Wi-Fi antenna to Wi-Fi connector.
    <img src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/Cudytech-pr/User-Guide/docs/images/industrial_router/install-antennas-ir02.webp"  width="500" alt="">

2. Screw the Wi-Fi antenna clockwise to tighten it.
    <img src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/Cudytech-pr/User-Guide/docs/images/industrial_router/install-antennas-ir022.webp"  width="500" alt="">

3. Align the connector on 4G antenna with that on the router. Rotate the nut clockwise onto the connector until tight. Then place it on the magnetic base.
    <img src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/Cudytech-pr/User-Guide/docs/images/industrial_router/install-antennas-ir023.webp"  width="500" alt="">

---

## Install the Router

Insert the screws through the four mounting holes and fasten them into the wall.
    <img src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/Cudytech-pr/User-Guide/docs/images/industrial_router/install-router-ir02.webp"  width="700" alt="">

---

## Power Supply

Method A: Connect a switching power supply to the 2-pin terminal block installed on the router (5~36 VDC).
    <img src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/Cudytech-pr/User-Guide/docs/images/industrial_router/power-ir02-a.webp"  width="500" alt="">

Method B: Connect the provided power adapter to the 2-pin terminal block installed on the router via the provided DC-to-terminal power cable.
    <img src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/Cudytech-pr/User-Guide/docs/images/industrial_router/power-ir02-b.webp"  width="500" alt="">
